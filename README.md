# Project Golf

Project Golf brings full-featured golf to Minecraft.

Build complete golf courses using dedicated tees, cups, fairways, putting greens, bunkers, slopes, and precision terrain pieces, then play them with a physics-driven golf system featuring multiple club types, wind, shot prediction, putting, scoring, multiplayer rounds, scorecards, leaderboards, and course flyovers.

### Features

- Driver, Woods, Irons, Wedges, Putters, and rare specialty clubs
- Physics-based golf balls with carry, roll, slopes, wind, terrain resistance, and hazards
- 16 dyeable golf-ball colors
- Full course creation and editing tools
- Tee markers, cups, guide points, and hole flyovers
- Fairway, fringe, rough, deep rough, putting green, bunker, slabs, layers, and slopes
- Multiplayer rounds and shared scoring
- Detailed physical scorecards awarded after rounds
- Persistent player history and course leaderboards
- Driving Range Tees for distance practice and target ranges
- Golf Bag equipment storage
- Golden Spotter for locating your ball, tee, and cup
- Course-selection and course-design GUIs
- Achievements and golf milestones
- Configurable gameplay, physics, visuals, wind, and penalties
- Designed for both survival courses and large multiplayer golf clubs

Project Golf requires Ritchies Projectile Library
